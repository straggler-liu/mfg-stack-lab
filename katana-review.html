Source-verified profile

Katana emphasizes inventory and manufacturing workflows connected to commerce and accounting systems. Its published Core price reviewed for this project starts at $299 per month, while usage, locations, add-ons and onboarding can affect the final quote.

Why it is in the test set

  • Strong Shopify and multichannel positioning.
  • Unlimited-user economics that contrast with per-user MRP pricing.
  • Production and purchasing workflows for growing product companies.

Required hands-on evidence

  • Shopify order and stock synchronization without duplicates.
  • Multi-level BOM and production-order handling.
  • QuickBooks Online transaction mapping.
  • Outsourced production and partial completion.
  • Full data export and three-year usage-based cost.

Provisional fit

Katana belongs on the shortlist for a manufacturer whose commercial stack starts with Shopify or multichannel inventory. Machine shops and complex routings require a deeper pilot before selection.
