MFGFIT/1 buyer triage model

The free Fit Check is a preliminary path-ranking model. Inputs include manufacturing model, current stack, sales channels, BOM depth, production-planning depth, traceability, locations, inventory reliability, QuickBooks/Shopify requirements, budget and decision timeline.

The current path set is Katana, MRPeasy, inFlow and an Intuit-centered path. The browser calculates scores locally. The model manifest and current source anchors are published in data/fit-model-v1.json.

Hard gates

  • Multi-level BOM: an Intuit-centered manufacturing path is capped because Intuit currently documents that multi-level BOMs with subassemblies are not supported in Intuit Enterprise Suite.
  • Mandatory traceability: feature-list matching is insufficient; a workflow-level pilot is required.
  • Unreliable opening inventory: migration is not recommended until item master and opening inventory can be reconciled.

Free Fit Check vs deep diagnostic

The free model narrows the decision set. The $149 deep diagnostic adds a case-specific Requirement Matrix, explicit fit/non-fit reasons, migration and integration risks, data priorities and a controlled 30-day pilot plan.

Commercial separation: score first, monetize second

For each buyer, the fit profile and recommendation order are generated before the system checks affiliate status, referral agreements, accepted-introduction fees or other seller relationships. The commercial layer may determine how a consented introduction is routed; it may not change the score.

  • Non-paying vendors remain eligible to rank first.
  • A paying vendor cannot suppress a better-fitting competitor.
  • Buyer consent is required before identifiable profile data is shared with a vendor.
  • A seller fee is recorded only when the relevant acceptance or referral condition is evidenced.
