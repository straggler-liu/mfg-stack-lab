Editorial method

How we test manufacturing software

Every conclusion must be tied to a dated source, a repeatable task or clearly marked as pending.

Evidence states

StatusMeaning
Source verifiedConfirmed on a current official vendor or primary source page and dated.
Hands-on testedCompleted against a standard dataset with screenshots, timing and exported evidence.
PendingNot yet tested; no score or definitive claim is published.

Two standard test companies

Northstar Foods

A 25-person direct-to-consumer food manufacturer with multi-level BOMs, Shopify, QuickBooks, lots, expiry dates and recall requirements.

Apex Precision

A 40-person make-to-order machine shop with routings, work centers, partial completion, subcontracting and actual-cost requirements.

Fifteen core tasks

  1. Company setup and defaults.
  2. Bulk item import.
  3. Multi-level BOM creation.
  4. Routing and work-center setup.
  5. Lot-controlled purchasing.
  6. Manufacturing-order creation.
  7. Partial completion and WIP.
  8. Subcontract operation.
  9. Shopify synchronization.
  10. QuickBooks mapping.
  11. Cost variance.
  12. Recall trace.
  13. Permissions.
  14. Core-data export.
  15. Support response.
