48-Hour MRP Decision & Implementation Risk Brief

For a real 0–90 day decision: moving beyond Excel or QuickBooks, comparing light MRP options, or deciding whether to stabilize an existing system before replacement.

$149 USD founding-customer priceIndependent written recommendationOptional 20-minute findings review
The outcome is a decision, not a software list. You receive a written go / conditional / not-ready view, the evidence behind it and the next 30 days of work. If the available facts cannot support a case-specific recommendation, we do not send a payment request.

Best fit

  • A manufacturer expects to make a software or implementation decision within 90 days.
  • Excel, QuickBooks, inventory software or an existing MRP/ERP no longer gives the team a reliable operating answer.
  • The decision owner can provide the current stack, primary constraint, production model and decision timing.
Not a fit: general research with no active decision, requests for implementation credentials, or projects requiring confidential production data before scope is agreed.

Deliverables

  • Readiness and complexity classification.
  • Requirement Matrix: must-have, should-have and non-critical requirements.
  • Shortlist of up to three software paths with explicit fit and non-fit reasons.
  • Top five implementation, integration and migration risks.
  • Data-readiness priorities for item master, BOM, inventory, purchasing and production.
  • 30-day pilot plan: what to test, what evidence must decide the final choice and what not to buy yet.

Commercial independence

MFG Stack Lab may receive referral, affiliate or qualified-introduction compensation from some vendors. That commercial check occurs only after the fit analysis is calculated. A paying vendor cannot buy a higher score, remove a better-fitting competitor or suppress a material limitation.

Delivery sequence

  1. Send the structured order request or a completed free Decision Map.
  2. We confirm the exact decision, evidence boundary and 48-hour deliverable. If the case is not suitable, no payment request is sent.
  3. You receive a buyer-specific Payoneer USD payment request. The 48-hour clock starts when payment is credited.
  4. We deliver the written brief and, if useful, one optional 20-minute findings review.
