Commercial policy

Affiliate and commercial disclosure

How commissions, free access and vendor relationships are handled.

Effective August 6, 2026

Launch status

MFG Stack Lab has no live affiliate tracking links, paid placements or vendor sponsorships at launch. Product links are ordinary source or navigation links.

Future commissions

The project may later earn a commission when a visitor follows a clearly identified commercial link and purchases or subscribes. Such links will carry appropriate sponsored or nofollow attributes and a plain-language disclosure near the recommendation.

What money cannot change

  • Which products enter the test set.
  • The standard companies, tasks or scoring weights.
  • Whether a limitation or failed task is published.
  • Whether a non-affiliate competitor is included.
  • The conclusion about who should avoid a product.

Free accounts and vendor access

Any free plan, extended trial, demo assistance or implementation support provided by a vendor will be disclosed on the relevant page. Access does not equal endorsement.

Current partner eligibility

Some programs may accept an individual publisher; others require a registered company, formal invoice or business payment account. The project will not misstate its legal status to obtain approval.

Corrections

Commercial or disclosure concerns can be submitted through the contact page.
