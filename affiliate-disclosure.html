Affiliate and commercial disclosure

How commissions and seller-side revenue are prevented from changing buyer recommendations.

Updated August 12, 2026
Money never changes Fit Score. MFG Stack Lab calculates and freezes buyer fit scores before checking whether a vendor has an affiliate, referral, lead-fee or other commercial relationship with us.

Current revenue paths

  • Buyer-paid: optional $149 Manufacturing Software Fit Diagnostic.
  • Vendor-paid: approved affiliate/referral commissions where a valid partner agreement exists.
  • Vendor-paid pilot: $99 for an accepted Qualified Buyer Introduction when no other agreement governs the referral and the vendor has agreed to the pilot terms.

Membership, sponsored rankings and aggregated intent-data products are not currently sold.

What money cannot change

  • Fit-model inputs, weights or hard gates for an individual buyer after that buyer submits a profile.
  • Recommendation order.
  • Whether a better-fitting non-paying competitor is shown.
  • Whether a tested limitation or failed task is published.
  • Whether a buyer can use the free Fit Check without accepting a vendor introduction.

Buyer consent before vendor sharing

MFG Stack Lab does not treat completion of the browser Fit Check as permission to share buyer data. A buyer must explicitly opt in to vendor introductions and send the generated profile. Only then may the profile be considered for a matching vendor introduction.

Seller acceptance and fees

A Qualified Buyer Introduction is chargeable only after the vendor accepts the opportunity under the applicable written terms. A duplicate, out-of-criteria, non-consented or unaccepted contact is not represented as seller revenue.

Affiliate and referral links

When a valid tracking link becomes active, the commercial relationship will be disclosed near the recommendation or outbound link. Links will be labeled appropriately. A normal vendor or source link is not described as an affiliate link unless attribution is actually active.

Free accounts and vendor access

Any free plan, extended trial, demo assistance or implementation support provided by a vendor will be disclosed on the relevant page. Access does not equal endorsement.

Legal-status honesty

Some programs require a registered company, formal invoice or specific payment account. MFG Stack Lab will not misstate its legal status, revenue, customer history or entity type to obtain approval.

Corrections

Commercial or disclosure concerns can be submitted through the contact page.
