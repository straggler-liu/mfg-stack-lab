Terms of use

Conditions for using the research, buyer diagnostic and vendor-introduction services.

Updated August 12, 2026

Independent project

MFG Stack Lab is currently operated as an independent research project and does not claim to be a limited company. References to the project, we or our mean the publisher during this validation stage.

Free Fit Check

The browser-based Manufacturing Software Fit Check is a preliminary decision aid. It narrows possible software paths using structured inputs and current evidence. It is not a vendor quote, implementation specification, accounting opinion, legal opinion, cybersecurity review or guarantee of product performance.

$149 Deep Diagnostic

The Manufacturing Software Fit Diagnostic is an optional written software-selection and migration-readiness service. Work begins only after scope and payment instructions are confirmed. The result depends on information supplied by the customer and evidence available at the time. It does not guarantee vendor acceptance, implementation success, savings or a particular commercial outcome.

Opt-in vendor introductions

A buyer may explicitly request introductions to matching vendors. By opting in and sending the generated profile, the buyer authorizes MFG Stack Lab to share the minimum relevant business contact and operating profile with matching software vendors for the purpose of arranging a product discussion, trial or demo. A buyer is not required to opt in to use the free Fit Check or buy the deep diagnostic.

Seller-side pilot

Vendor opportunities are offered only under written commercial terms. The current pilot may use an approved affiliate/referral agreement or a $99 accepted Qualified Buyer Introduction. A fee is not treated as due or earned until the applicable acceptance condition is evidenced. Duplicates, out-of-criteria, non-consented or rejected contacts are not represented as qualified-introduction revenue.

No pay-to-rank

Vendors cannot purchase a higher buyer Fit Score, removal of a competitor, suppression of a tested limitation or access to non-consented buyer data. Commercial relationships are checked after scoring.

Payments and cancellation

Payment method, timing and any cancellation/refund terms are provided before paid work begins or a seller-side opportunity is accepted. Where an approved vendor partner agreement exists, that agreement controls the applicable referral relationship.

Customer information

Users must not send passwords, API keys, confidential customer lists, regulated records or other credentials. Use only the business information needed to make the software decision.

Verification and change

Software prices, features, limits, integrations and partner terms can change. Material facts should be verified directly with the vendor before purchase, data migration or contract signature.

The site is provided on an as-available basis. Reasonable efforts are made to cite and date material claims, but completeness, uninterrupted availability and error-free operation are not guaranteed.

Changes and contact

These terms may be revised as payment rails, partner approvals, analytics or legal-entity status change. Questions can be submitted through the project contact page.
