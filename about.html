About MFG Stack Lab

A validation-stage research project helping small manufacturers choose software from operating evidence rather than vendor claims.

Why this project exists

Most software lists compare feature labels. Manufacturing buyers need to understand whether a system can execute their BOM, routing, traceability, accounting and migration workflow without creating a second spreadsheet system.

Current legal status

MFG Stack Lab is currently an independent, non-incorporated research project. It is not presented as a limited company. A corporate entity may be formed only after the business demonstrates real traffic, referrals or paid demand.

Editorial promise

  • Separate official-source facts from hands-on conclusions.
  • Do not publish a score before completing the standard tasks.
  • Include relevant non-affiliate competitors.
  • State who should not buy each product.
  • Date prices and terms.
  • Correct material errors transparently.

Validation stage

The first objective is to determine whether small manufacturers use the tools, search for the research and proceed to vendor trials. No affiliate tracking, advertising analytics or email collection is active at launch.
