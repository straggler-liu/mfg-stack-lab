Accounting → Manufacturing

Keep Xero for accounting—or move more of manufacturing into another system?

The useful question is not whether Xero is “enough.” It is which operational decisions belong outside accounting.

Independent decision guide. Commercial relationships do not change fit scoring or recommendation order.

Start with the system boundary

Xero can remain the accounting ledger while another system handles inventory, bills of materials, work orders, material planning and production control. Replacing accounting is not automatically required just because manufacturing operations have outgrown spreadsheets.

Operating problemLikely boundary
Stock visibility, purchasing, barcode/warehouse control, kits or light assemblyInventory layer may be enough
Dependent demand, multi-level BOMs, work orders, material shortages, WIP or production schedulingMRP/manufacturing layer becomes more important
Multi-entity finance, intercompany, broad enterprise workflows, project/service accounting or tightly unified global controlsBroader ERP may be justified

Five signals that accounting + spreadsheets is becoming the constraint

  1. Purchasing decisions depend on manual BOM explosions or spreadsheet formulas.
  2. Material shortages are discovered after jobs are released.
  3. WIP cannot be reconciled quickly to orders and inventory.
  4. One change to demand forces multiple people to update separate files.
  5. Job costing, traceability or production status requires manual reconstruction.

Do not replace Xero without a reason

If finance is working and the primary failure is production planning, inventory control or shop-floor visibility, an integrated manufacturing layer may be lower risk than replacing the accounting ledger. A broader ERP becomes more defensible only when the finance/enterprise boundary itself is part of the problem.

The two questions that usually change the decision

1. How complex is production? Simple assembly/kitting, single-level BOM/work orders, or multi-level BOMs with material/capacity scheduling?

2. What is forcing the decision now? Inventory accuracy, purchasing/material shortages, scheduling/WIP, traceability/quality, job costing, ecommerce fulfillment, or broader ERP integration?

What a safe pilot should prove

  • A representative order can move from demand through purchasing/production to shipment without spreadsheet reconciliation.
  • Inventory and WIP agree with an approved physical/financial baseline.
  • BOM changes and purchasing suggestions are traceable.
  • The accounting handoff to Xero is clear and repeatable.
  • Users can export core operational data before committing to a full rollout.
