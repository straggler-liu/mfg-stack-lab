Corrections and research

Contact the project

Report a factual error, changed price, missing competitor or test scenario that would materially improve the research.

Current contact channel

During the public validation stage, use the GitHub repository's Issues tab for transparent, trackable requests:

Open a GitHub issue

Useful submissions

  • A current official source showing a changed price, limit or partner term.
  • A reproducible error in a tool or page.
  • A manufacturing workflow that the standard test fails to cover.
  • A correction to product positioning or terminology.

Do not submit

Do not post private customer data, credentials, confidential contracts or personally identifying production records in a public issue. Use anonymized examples.

Response policy

Material factual errors are prioritized. Vendor requests do not receive preferential scoring, and product inclusion cannot be purchased.
