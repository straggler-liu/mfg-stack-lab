From Excel to MRP without losing control

A practical, evidence-led migration sequence for small manufacturers.

Do not migrate a broken spreadsheet

Software does not repair unclear item ownership, duplicate SKUs, unmaintained BOMs or undocumented workarounds. The migration should first create a controlled operating model, then move that model into software.

Eight-step migration sequence

  1. Define scope: one site, one product family and one owner.
  2. Freeze naming: unique SKU, item type, UOM and status.
  3. Clean relationships: suppliers, customers, locations and lead times.
  4. Validate BOMs: quantities, scrap, version, effective date and subassemblies.
  5. Decide WIP treatment: finish, cancel or reconstruct open work.
  6. Count opening inventory: create a dated, signed physical baseline.
  7. Pilot end-to-end: purchase, receive, build, ship, invoice and trace.
  8. Cut over with rollback: retain read-only legacy access and reconciliation checkpoints.

Cutover controls

  • Total inventory value reconciles to the approved baseline.
  • Every active finished good has an approved BOM and revision.
  • Open orders reconcile by count and value.
  • At least one lot or serial can be traced end-to-end.
  • Core reports are reproducible by two trained users.
  • Exports have been tested before the old system is retired.

Pilot acceptance

Do not define success as “the software is configured.” Define it as one representative order completed with correct material, schedule, cost, accounting and traceability outcomes—and a team capable of repeating it.
