Project policy

Privacy notice

What the static validation website does and does not collect.

Effective August 6, 2026

Current data collection

MFG Stack Lab does not operate an account system, lead form, newsletter, advertising tracker or visitor analytics service at launch.

Interactive tools

The MRP readiness assessment and TCO calculator run in the visitor's browser. Answers and calculations are not transmitted to an MFG Stack Lab server.

GitHub Pages hosting

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information for security, reliability and abuse prevention under its own privacy terms. This project does not receive visitor-level server logs from the static site.

Outbound links

External vendors and source sites may collect information under their own policies after a visitor follows a link. No affiliate tracking links are active at launch.

Cookies

The current project does not intentionally set non-essential cookies or persistent local storage.

Future changes

Before analytics, email collection, affiliate tracking or paid services are enabled, this notice will be updated to identify the provider, purpose, information collected and contact route.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the project contact page.
