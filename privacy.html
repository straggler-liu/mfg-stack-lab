Project policy

Privacy notice

What the website and commercial email intake collect and how that information is used.

Updated August 10, 2026

Website data collection

MFG Stack Lab does not currently operate an account system, advertising tracker or first-party visitor analytics service. The public MRP readiness assessment and TCO calculator run in the visitor's browser and do not transmit answers to an MFG Stack Lab server.

Commercial email intake

If you email the project to request a paid diagnostic or make a business inquiry, the message and the information you include are processed through Gmail so the project can evaluate the request, reply, maintain order records and deliver the requested service. Do not send credentials, confidential customer lists or regulated production records.

Information you choose to provide

A diagnostic request may include business contact information and operating context such as company name, country, team size, manufacturing type, current software, sales channels, approximate SKU count and workflow problems. Provide only information necessary for the diagnostic.

GitHub Pages hosting

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information for security, reliability and abuse prevention under its own privacy terms. This project does not currently receive visitor-level server logs from the static site.

Outbound links

External vendors and source sites may collect information under their own policies after a visitor follows a link. No affiliate tracking link is published unless the project has a valid partner link and the commercial relationship is disclosed.

Cookies

The current project does not intentionally set non-essential cookies or persistent local storage.

Retention and deletion requests

Commercial emails and working records may be retained as needed to manage an inquiry, order, delivery, dispute or financial record. A person may request deletion of non-required inquiry information by contacting the project, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Future changes

Before adding a new analytics, CRM, newsletter or payment provider, this notice will be updated to identify the relevant purpose and data flow where appropriate.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the project contact page.
