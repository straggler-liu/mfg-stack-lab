Project policy

Privacy notice

What the site, buyer intake and vendor-introduction process collect and how that information is used.

Updated August 12, 2026

Browser-based Fit Check

The free Manufacturing Software Fit Check runs in the visitor's browser. Completing or calculating the questionnaire does not by itself transmit answers to MFG Stack Lab. A generated email is sent only if the visitor chooses to send it through their email client.

Information a buyer may choose to send

A Fit Check or diagnostic request can include business contact details and operating context such as company name, work email, company-size band, manufacturing model, current software, sales channels, BOM/planning complexity, traceability, locations, inventory condition, required integrations, budget band and decision timeline. Do not send passwords, API keys, confidential customer lists or regulated production records.

How buyer information is used

Buyer information may be used to reply to the inquiry, generate or review a fit note, provide a paid diagnostic if ordered, maintain lead/order records, prevent duplicate outreach and measure aggregate commercial-funnel performance.

Vendor introductions require explicit opt-in

MFG Stack Lab does not share an identifiable buyer profile with a software vendor merely because the buyer used the Fit Check. A buyer must explicitly opt in to vendor introductions. When the buyer opts in and sends the profile, MFG Stack Lab may share the minimum relevant business profile with one or more matching software vendors for the purpose of arranging a product discussion, trial or demo. MFG Stack Lab may receive referral, affiliate or qualified-introduction compensation if the relationship is valid and disclosed.

No pay-to-rank data use

Commercial relationships are checked after fit scoring. Buyer data is not used to secretly raise a paying vendor's score or remove a better-fitting non-paying competitor.

Commercial email and working records

Business inquiries are processed through Gmail. Structured lead, outreach, partner and revenue records may be maintained in internal Google Sheets for operating the service, attribution, duplicate prevention, seller acceptance and evidence of actual revenue.

GitHub Pages hosting

The public site is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information for security, reliability and abuse prevention under its own terms. MFG Stack Lab does not currently operate first-party visitor analytics or advertising trackers on the static site.

Cookies and local storage

The current MFG Stack Lab site does not intentionally set non-essential cookies or persistent local storage. External vendor sites may do so under their own policies after a visitor follows a link.

Retention and deletion

Commercial emails and working records may be retained as needed to manage inquiries, orders, delivery, duplicate controls, disputes, seller acceptance and financial records. A person may request deletion of non-required inquiry data, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Questions

Privacy questions can be submitted through the project contact page.
