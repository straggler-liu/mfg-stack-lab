Xero · manufacturing · MRP · inventory

Keep Xero for accounting—or add a manufacturing system?

For many small manufacturers, the decision is not whether to replace Xero. It is whether production, inventory, BOMs, WIP, purchasing and costing have outgrown an accounting-led stack.

Run the 90-sec Quick FitGet a reviewed shortlist
Stay lightweight

Xero is not the bottleneck

If inventory is reliable, BOMs are simple and production planning is informal but controlled, adding full MRP may create more work than value.

Add manufacturing depth

Operations have outgrown accounting

Recurring shortages, multi-level BOMs, work orders, routings, WIP, capacity or job-costing gaps are signs to test a dedicated manufacturing layer.

Do not assume integration depth

Accounting sync is not all-or-nothing

Different manufacturing systems send different data to Xero. Test invoices, bills, tax mapping, inventory/WIP journals and multi-currency behavior before committing.

Current official integration boundaries

Katana and MRPeasy both support Xero—but the workflows differ.

These are product-documentation facts, not implementation guarantees. Reverify before purchase because integrations change.

Decision areaKatana + XeroMRPeasy + Xero
Sales / purchasingKatana can create Xero invoices from sales orders and bills from purchase orders.MRPeasy supports synchronization of purchasing and sales documents, with automatic or manual options.
Inventory accountingKatana documents that stock levels are not automatically synced to Xero balance-sheet inventory accounts; manual journal handling may be needed.MRPeasy documents an optional deeper integration that can post inventory and manufacturing journal entries, including WIP and COGS.

Representative pilot

Test one order end to end before migrating.

  1. Create one real customer order and product/BOM.
  2. Buy and receive one constrained material.
  3. Release and partially complete production.
  4. Record WIP, scrap/rework or labor if those matter.
  5. Finish and ship the order.
  6. Push the accounting documents to Xero.
  7. Check tax, currency, inventory/WIP and COGS treatment with your accountant.
  8. Export the core data you would need if you later leave the system.
