inFlow Manufacturing review

Testing whether an inventory-first platform is sufficient for light manufacturing.

Hands-on test pendingOfficial sources checked August 6, 2026
No score yet. This page remains noindex until BOM, manufacturing-order, warehouse and export tests are complete.

Source-verified profile

inFlow combines inventory, warehouse and order management with manufacturing functions. Published annual-billing prices reviewed for this project begin at $179 per month for the Manufacturing Start Up plan, with higher tiers and add-ons materially affecting cost.

Why it is in the test set

  • Inventory and warehouse strength with manufacturing extensions.
  • Barcode and order-volume economics.
  • A potential step between spreadsheets and full MRP.

Required hands-on evidence

  • BOM and manufacture-order workflow.
  • Material and labor costing.
  • Lot, serial, warehouse and barcode controls.
  • User, order, integration and add-on cost model.
  • Core data export and exit risk.

Provisional fit

Include inFlow when inventory accuracy, fulfillment and purchasing are the dominant constraints. A manufacturer with complex routing, capacity or subcontracting should compare it against a fuller MRP product.
