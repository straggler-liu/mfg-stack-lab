Why “cheapest monthly price” can be misleading

A five-user manufacturer can see a different cost ranking from a twenty-user manufacturer. A commerce-heavy company can be more sensitive to sales-order or integration economics, while a job shop may care more about whether the selected tier actually covers routing, costing and production control. The right comparison is therefore:

  1. List hard requirements first.
  2. Eliminate systems that fail a hard requirement.
  3. Price only the surviving paths.
  4. Add implementation, migration, integration and training costs.
  5. Compare the first-year and three-year TCO against staying on the current stack.
