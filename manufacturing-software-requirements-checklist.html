Manufacturing software requirements checklist

Separate hard gates from nice-to-have features before vendor demos begin.

1. Operating model

  • Make-to-stock, make-to-order, engineer-to-order, batch or mixed?
  • In-house, outsourced or contract manufacturing?
  • Single site or multiple warehouses/plants?

2. Product and BOM control

  • No BOM, single-level, multi-level or deep subassemblies?
  • Revision/effectivity control required?
  • Alternates, substitutes, phantom/kit structures?

3. Planning and execution

  • Work orders only, or routings/work centers/capacity scheduling?
  • Finite scheduling or simple priority sequencing?
  • Scrap, rework, subcontracting and WIP visibility?

4. Purchasing and inventory

  • Reorder points versus MRP-driven purchasing?
  • Lot/serial/expiry traceability?
  • Multi-location transfers and cycle counting?

5. Costing and finance

  • Standard, average, FIFO or job costing?
  • Must accounting remain in QuickBooks/Xero?
  • What data must reconcile at month end?

6. Commerce and integrations

  • Shopify, Amazon, wholesale, EDI or direct B2B?
  • Which system must be the source of truth for items, customers, inventory and orders?

7. Migration readiness

  • Are item masters, BOMs, vendor records and opening inventory reliable?
  • Can one representative product/order family be piloted before full migration?

8. Buying constraints

  • Budget band, decision timeline, required users and implementation owner?
  • Which three requirements would immediately disqualify a vendor?
