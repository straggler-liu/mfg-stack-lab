Qualified manufacturing software opportunities

MFG Stack Lab turns buyer workflow facts into opt-in software opportunities with explicit fit logic and purchase timing.

No pay-to-rankBuyer consent requiredPilot terms · Aug 2026
Core rule: Money never changes Fit Score. Buyer requirements are scored and the recommendation order is frozen before any affiliate, referral, lead-fee or vendor relationship is checked.

What a Qualified Opportunity contains

  • Company and verified business contact path supplied by the buyer.
  • Manufacturing model and company-size band.
  • Current operating stack and required accounting/ecommerce integrations.
  • BOM, planning, traceability, location and inventory-complexity profile.
  • Budget band and decision timeline.
  • Preliminary fit score and the operating reasons behind the match.
  • Explicit buyer consent to a vendor introduction.

Acceptance standard

An opportunity is seller-chargeable only when the buyer has opted in, the profile meets agreed vendor criteria, the contact is not known to be a duplicate or already-active opportunity, and MFG Stack Lab provides the structured buyer profile. A vendor can reject an introduction as duplicate or out of criteria before accepting it.

What vendors cannot buy

  • A higher Fit Score.
  • Removal or downgrading of a better-fitting competitor.
  • Suppression of a tested limitation.
  • Buyer data without the buyer's explicit vendor-introduction consent.
  • Unlabeled sponsored placement inside an independent ranking.

Current pilot focus

The initial buyer universe is small US manufacturers, especially Shopify manufacturers, make-to-order/job shops, contract manufacturers and QuickBooks-centered product businesses considering inventory or MRP software.
